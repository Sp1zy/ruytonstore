About us

The village shop on Church Street

Draft copy – to confirm with the shop

Ruyton XI Towns Store is the village shop for Ruyton-XI-Towns and the surrounding area, offering local produce alongside everyday convenience.

We work with quality local providers, including Appleyards’ famous bacon, sausages and cheese, Corbett’s butchers and game, and Niche Patisseries, so you can buy local without leaving the village.

This website lets you pre-order produce and pick it up from the shop.

Our suppliers

Most of what’s on our shelves comes from producers within a few miles of the shop.

  • AppleyardsBacon · sausages · cheese

    Famous for their bacon and sausages, plus the cheeses that go into our cheese boards.

  • Corbett'sButchers & game specialist

    Aged beef joints and seasonal game, ready to pre-order for the weekend.

  • Niche PatisseriesCakes & patisserie

    Freshly made cakes and sweet treats, delivered to the shop.

  • Eden Honey BeesRaw local honey · Myddle

    Raw, unpasteurised honey from apiaries around North Shropshire, including Shotatton just down the road.
